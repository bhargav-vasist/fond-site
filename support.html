Common questions

Did Fond delete my photo?

Only after you choose Review and delete and confirm with iOS. Open Recently Deleted in Apple Photos to get a photo back within 30 days.

Fond cannot see my photos.

Open Settings, then Fond, then Photos, and allow access to all photos or select some.

How do I undo a swipe?

Tap Undo on the deck. Photos in the trash tray can also be put back from the review screen.
