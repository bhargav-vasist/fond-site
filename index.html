Your photos never leave your iPhone

Fond reads and sorts your photos on your iPhone. It has no account and no ads, and nobody else sees your photos, not even the developer.

  • Fond finds where faces are, to crop around them. It doesn't know who anyone is.
  • What you keep or delete is saved on your iPhone.
  • Nothing is deleted until you review and delete, and iOS asks you first.
