Privacy policy

Fond: Photo Cleanup & Memories. Last updated 15 November 2026.

Your photos never leave your phone. Fond reads and sorts your photo library on your iPhone. Nobody else sees your photos, not even the developer.

What Fond collects

Anonymous usage data, and nothing about your photos or about you. It helps the developer see where people get stuck on the first day, how well the app runs, and whether people come back. Fond has no account, no ads and no tracking.

What is counted:

  • When the app is opened, and the steps of the first run, such as whether photo access was allowed.
  • When you review and delete, the number of photos and their size as a range, such as "10 to 49 photos".
  • How the app runs, from Apple's MetricKit: how long it takes to open, how often it stops responding, how smooth scrolling is, and how many times it crashed. Only the numbers, never the crash reports.
  • The app version, the iOS version and the iPhone model.
  • A random number made when Fond is installed, so the same install can be counted on different days. It is scrambled on your phone before it is sent, it isn't linked to you, and it is gone when you delete Fond.

What is never counted: your photos or videos, anything read from them, their dates, places or file names, your name, your email or your contacts.

The data goes to TelemetryDeck, an analytics service based in Germany with servers in Germany and the Netherlands. TelemetryDeck doesn't store IP addresses. The data isn't sold or used for advertising.

Turning it off: Profile, "Share anonymous usage data". It is on until you turn it off, and nothing is sent after that.

What stays on your phone

  • The choices you make while swiping (keep, delete, decide later). They are saved on your phone so Fond remembers where you left off.
  • Your photos and videos stay in Apple Photos. Fond shows them and asks iOS to delete the ones you choose.
  • What Fond works out from your photos, such as which ones look alike and where faces are. Fond finds where a face is, to crop around it. It doesn't know who anyone is.

Deleting photos

Fond never deletes a photo from a single swipe. Photos you swipe left on wait in the trash tray. When you choose Review and delete, iOS asks you to confirm, and deleted photos go to Recently Deleted in Apple Photos.

Photo access

Fond asks for access to your photo library. You can allow all photos or only selected ones, and you can change this at any time in Settings.

iCloud

If a photo is stored in iCloud, iOS downloads it to your phone when Fond needs to show it. That is handled by Apple, not by Fond.

Place names

If a photo has a location, Fond shows where it was taken, such as "Brooklyn". To get that name, it sends the photo's location, and nothing else, to Apple Maps. The photo itself is never sent. Fond doesn't store the names, and the developer can't see these requests. Apple's handling is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Fond collects no personal data from anyone, including children. The anonymous usage data above is the same for everyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date.
