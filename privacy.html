Privacy policy

Fond: Photo Cleanup & Memories. Last updated 7 November 2026.

Your photos never leave your phone. Fond reads your photo library on your iPhone, and everything it does happens there.

What Fond collects

Nothing. Fond does not collect or share any data about you or your photos. It has no account, no ads and no third party analytics or tracking. The only thing it sends anywhere is described under Place names.

What stays on your phone

  • The choices you make while swiping (keep, delete, decide later). They are saved on your phone so Fond remembers where you left off.
  • Your photos and videos stay in Apple Photos. Fond shows them and asks iOS to delete the ones you choose.

Deleting photos

Fond never deletes a photo from a single swipe. Photos you swipe left on wait in the trash tray. When you choose Review and delete, iOS asks you to confirm, and deleted photos go to Recently Deleted in Apple Photos.

Photo access

Fond asks for access to your photo library. You can allow all photos or only selected ones, and you can change this at any time in Settings.

iCloud

If a photo is stored in iCloud, iOS downloads it to your phone when Fond needs to show it. That is handled by Apple, not by Fond.

Place names

If a photo has a location, Fond shows where it was taken, such as "Brooklyn". To get that name, it sends the photo's location, and nothing else, to Apple Maps. The photo itself is never sent. Fond doesn't store the names, and the developer can't see these requests. Apple's handling is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Fond does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date.
