Privacy questions

Fond: Photo Cleanup & Memories

Your photos never leave your iPhone. Here are the short answers, then a little more on how it works.

Short answers

Do my photos leave my iPhone?

No. Fond reads and sorts your photos on your iPhone. It has no servers to send them to.

Can anyone else see my photos?

No. Not other people, not the developer.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign in, and no ads.

Does Fond know who is in my photos?

No. It finds where faces are in a photo, to crop around them. It doesn't know who anyone is.

Can Fond delete photos by itself?

No. Nothing is deleted until you choose Review and delete, and iOS asks you first.

Does Fond collect usage data?

Not today. Fond sends no usage data. If that changes, this page and the privacy policy will say exactly what is counted first.

How it works

Reading your photos

Fond reads your library through Apple's Photos framework, on your iPhone. You can give it all your photos or only some, and change that at any time in Settings.

Duplicates and memories

Fond compares photos with Apple's Vision framework, on your iPhone, to find ones that look alike. Memories, such as On this day, are picked from the dates of your photos. What Fond works out stays in its own files on your phone.

Faces

Fond asks Vision where the faces are in a photo, so a cropped photo keeps them in view. It finds where a face is, not whose face it is.

Your choices

What you keep, delete or decide later is saved on your iPhone, so Fond remembers where you left off. There is no account to send it to. Delete Fond and they go with it.

Photos in iCloud

When a photo is only in iCloud, Apple's Photos downloads it to your iPhone, the same way the Photos app does. That download is between your iPhone and your own iCloud.

Deleting

Photos you swipe left on wait in the trash tray. Review and delete removes them in one batch, after iOS asks you to confirm. Deleted photos stay in Recently Deleted in Apple Photos for 30 days.

The daily memory

The daily memory is a notification your iPhone schedules for itself. There is no push server behind it.

How we make sure

Keeping your photos on your iPhone is the first rule for everyone who works on Fond. Every change to the app is checked automatically, and the check fails if the code starts using an internet service it isn't allowed to.
